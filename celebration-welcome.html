What Happens Next

The journey from signed to rented

From this moment forward, you have a small, dedicated team behind your property. Here's what to expect — and who you'll hear from at each step.

01
The Onboarding Form
From Pamela

You'll receive the New Client Onboarding Form next — it captures the property details we'll use to build out your AppFolio file, write your listing, and prepare your eventual lease. It takes 15–20 minutes; feel free to save and return.

02
The Property Visit
From Linda (or our Partner Vendor)

This is where the warmth of Verandah shows up at your door. Linda Lopez, our New Client Liaison, will visit the property in person if it's in our Lake Nona service area — to walk through with you, capture details for marketing, and meet you face to face.

For properties outside Lake Nona, our trusted partner vendor will handle the walkthrough on Verandah's behalf, and Linda will stay in close coordination with you throughout.

03
Your Owner Portal
From Pamela · with Megan on standby

You'll receive an activation link to your AppFolio Owner Portal — your secure window into every statement, document, and disbursement we generate on your behalf. A complete setup guide will walk you through activation.

04
The Rently Lockbox
From Pamela · with Megan on standby

We'll install a Rently smart lockbox at your property so we can begin showings the moment you're ready. You'll receive a full owner guide walking through the setup. Until you physically place a house key inside, no one can enter your property.

05
Marketing Goes Live
From Pamela

The moment your property is rent-ready and we've gathered marketing materials, your listing goes live across the major rental platforms. You'll receive a note from us confirming the launch — with a link to the live listing.

06
Showings, Feedback & Applications
From Linda

From listing through tenant placement, Linda is your direct line. Weekly updates on showings, feedback themes, market signals, and incoming applications — you'll never wonder what's happening with your property.

07
Lease Signed → Handoff to Client Experience
From Linda → Megan & Luz

The moment a lease is signed, your day-to-day contact shifts to Megan Fairchild and Luz Reyes on our Client Experience team. They'll own the ongoing relationship — statements, maintenance, contributions, anything operational — for the long term.
